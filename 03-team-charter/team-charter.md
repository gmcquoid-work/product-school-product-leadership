# Team Charter: Meridian Foundations

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** The three Now bets and their success signals: in-app photo markup (field-originated items from 6% toward 15%), two-way notifications (80% of field items actioned within 24 hours), and the 4-field daily log (median completion under 2 minutes). We also own the engineering capacity ring-fenced to those bets in Field Experience, Platform/Notifications and Office Workflows for the quarter, and the go/no-go on starting any Next item that depends on Now adoption data.
>
> **Out of scope:** Renewal-driven and customer-specific enterprise requests belong to PM 2 and Enterprise Sales, and are funded from enterprise capacity, never from ring-fenced Foundations capacity. Subcontractor sharing (Platform, Security, Partnerships), scheduling integrations (Integrations), and AI tagging and risk signals (AI/ML, Data & Analytics) are not ours this quarter. Office reporting definitions stay with Office Workflows.
>
> **Joint calls:** PM 1, PM 2 and the Foundations owner must all agree before any of these goes ahead: moving any engineer off a Now bet for more than 3 days, adding or removing a daily log field, or changing which office events trigger a field notification. The same applies to any customer commitment that touches the mobile app or field workflows. Sales may not quote dates for any of these until the joint call is made.

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** PM 1 decides feature and scope calls on field capture, markup and the mobile experience, and PM 2 decides on office and enterprise reporting needs. Office Workflows can block a daily log change only by showing a specific enterprise report it would break. Only the Foundations owner can trade a Now KR for other work or change the ring-fenced capacity, which makes the Foundations owner the protector of the field bet.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** Whoever is losing capacity raises the conflict in writing to the Foundations owner within 1 working day of the request. The write-up states the renewal's ARR and deadline, the engineer-weeks requested, and which success signal slips. The Foundations owner decides within 2 working days, and if the request needs capacity beyond Foundations' allocation, the Head of Product decides with the Sales lead within 5 working days; until then, no engineer moves off the field bet.
